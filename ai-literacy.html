The Problem

Your staff are already using AI.

Unevenly, often without guidance, and sometimes without discussing it openly — while AI features arrive built into platforms your organization already uses.

These tools can fabricate quotations and sources in the same confident language they use for accurate information. They can distort Jewish history and text, expose confidential information, blur authorship, and create serious reputational risk.

An organization that transmits Jewish knowledge cannot afford staff who can't tell fluent output from accurate output.

The Difference

Why a Jewish-specific training?

Jewish communal work depends on accurate attribution, responsible interpretation, inherited memory, and trust between people and institutions. AI disrupts each of those practices. This training uses Jewish texts and learning traditions not as decoration, but as working tools for source integrity, disagreement, transmission, and human accountability.

Attribution

Repeating teachings in the name of their source — the discipline that keeps transmission trustworthy.

Humility

Learning to say "I do not know" — the discipline these tools were never taught.

Machloket

Disagreement as a discipline rather than automatic affirmation — what Jewish learning asks of a partner.

Common Questions

Before you ask

Do participants need AI experience?

No. The workshop is designed for mixed-comfort staff teams — from daily users to people who have never opened one of these tools.

Which AI tools are covered?

The training focuses on transferable judgment rather than promoting one platform. Demonstrations may include widely used tools and AI features already present in organizational software.

Do we need an existing AI policy?

No. The 90-minute workshop can help identify what the organization needs to clarify next. The half-day and implementation series support deeper organizational guidance.

Is this legal, cybersecurity, or compliance advice?

No. The training helps staff develop sound judgment and identify questions that should be escalated. Organizations should involve their own legal, IT, security, HR, and policy professionals where appropriate.

Will we have to share confidential materials?

No. Scenarios can reflect your work without requiring confidential constituent, donor, student, personnel, or pastoral information.

Can this be delivered virtually?

Yes. The virtual workshop is designed as a fully interactive format — polls, chat exercises, and paired investigation — not a reduced version of the on-site session.

How many participants does standard pricing cover?

Standard workshop pricing is designed for one organizational team. Larger multi-agency or conference audiences are quoted separately.
